# Results and limits

Reyn-Pair is an experimental Fox 2v2 model. It accepts 102 cached language instructions with shared or individual addressing. The command reference includes movement, platforms, attacks, defense and team behavior; it is a list of available instructions, not a guarantee that every request will succeed.

Throws and combos need the right opening. Platform requests need a stage with the requested platform. Each Fox can hold a different instruction, and their instructions can be changed or cleared independently. Other characters, unrestricted paraphrases and voice input have not been established by these tests.

## Friendly fire and the showcase

Teammate damage remains a limitation. The optional friendly-fire mask blocks attack inputs near a hittable teammate. It is a proximity heuristic, not a hitbox predictor or a guarantee of zero teammate damage. It can also block useful attacks.

The results above and the four continuous showcase clips used the mask off. The clips were selected to show the instructions and addressing clearly; they do not estimate typical success.
